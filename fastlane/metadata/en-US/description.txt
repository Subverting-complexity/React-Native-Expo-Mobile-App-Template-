Open with one or two sentences about who the app is for and the problem it solves. Write to the reader, not about the product.

Follow with a short paragraph explaining how the app works in plain language: what the user does, what happens next, and what they get out of it.

• Third feature, with a number where a number exists (e.g. "loops from 1–30 seconds")
